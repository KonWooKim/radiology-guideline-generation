# Human v1 and v2: policy summaries

These are concise editorial summaries, not the original documents or verbatim
experimental prompts. Both documents were written by the expert who annotated
both rounds. Neither was an input to LLM guideline generation or refinement.

| Area | Human v1, after the first 12 reports | Human v2, after the remaining 38 reports |
|---|---|---|
| Spans | Prefer word-level annotation units. | Add exceptions for measurements with units, multidimensional measurements, and hyphenated words. |
| Exclusions | Several exclusion decisions remain tentative. | Explicitly address placeholders, pronouns, patient references, and examination names. |
| Entity roles | Devices and body spaces are discussed tentatively. | Consolidate their treatment and distinguish organ features, properties, and fluids. Some border and tip decisions remain open. |
| Certainty | Absence cues and modifier certainty are unresolved. | Exclude cue words and specify target certainty, normality constructions, and certainty agreement for observation modifiers. |
| Context and relations | General semantic-head direction and tentative part-of treatment. | Add context-dependent temporal and comparative rules, attribute-to-possessor attachment, and a negative SuggestiveOf example. |

Discussion occurred after Human v1 and before round 2. Human v2 was written after
round 2, so document differences alone cannot determine when policies changed.
The original first-round annotations were not updated after discussion.
Human v1 was an evaluation comparator. Human v2 was contextual documentation, not
a model annotation condition. These summaries are not a consensus guideline.
